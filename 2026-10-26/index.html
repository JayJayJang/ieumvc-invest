About소개

IEUM Daily Investment Trends is prepared by IEUM Value Creator, a Seoul-based biopharma business-development and investor-relations advisory. The brief applies a liquidity-first macro framework — central-bank policy, the dollar and oil before earnings — to the markets Korean biotech investors actually trade: Nasdaq, HKEX and Kosdaq. Passages labelled "IEUM's read" are our own interpretation; everything else is sourced and timestamped.IEUM Daily Investment Trends는 서울 소재 바이오제약 사업개발·IR 자문사 IEUM Value Creator가 작성합니다. 실적보다 중앙은행·달러·유가를 먼저 보는 유동성 우선 프레임워크를, 국내 바이오 투자자가 실제로 거래하는 나스닥·HKEX·코스닥에 적용합니다.
